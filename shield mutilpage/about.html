Our Story

Shield Group was built around a simple belief: great projects should not be overlooked simply because they lack the right marketing strategy.

Too often, talented authors and capable publishers create exceptional work that never reaches the audiences most likely to value it. Not because the work isn't worthy, but because the path from creation to discovery is broken.

We founded Shield Group to fix that path. Our agency helps authors and publishers create stronger connections between their projects and the readers who are searching for exactly what they've created.

Through research, strategy, content, advertising, optimization, and reporting, we build systematic approaches to visibility, attention, and conversion—protecting your marketing investment while pursuing measurable growth.

"We build the path between attention and action."
