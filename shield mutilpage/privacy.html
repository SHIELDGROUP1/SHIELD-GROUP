Privacy Policy

How Shield Group collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: January 2026
Template Notice: This Privacy Policy is a starting framework prepared for Shield Group's website. It should be reviewed and finalized by a qualified attorney familiar with applicable data protection laws (including GDPR, CCPA, and relevant regulations in the US, UK, Australia, France, and Kuwait) before being relied upon as a legally binding document.

1. Introduction

Shield Group ("we," "us," "our") operates as a digital marketing agency serving authors and publishers internationally. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you visit our website or engage our services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact details you provide (name, email, WhatsApp number, country)
  • Project and business information submitted through our application form (book/project details, genre, goals, budget range, timeline)
  • Communication records when you contact us directly
  • Basic technical data such as browser type, device type, and general usage analytics

3. How We Use Information

Information collected is used to:

  • Evaluate and respond to project applications and inquiries
  • Prepare custom strategy recommendations and proposals
  • Communicate with clients and prospective clients
  • Improve our website, services, and internal processes
  • Comply with legal and contractual obligations

4. Client Confidentiality

Shield Group treats client project details, campaign performance, and business information as confidential. We do not publicly disclose client identities, proprietary campaign data, or sensitive business information without explicit permission. Case studies referenced on our website are anonymized by design.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with trusted service providers (such as email or scheduling tools) strictly to operate our business, and only under confidentiality obligations. We may disclose information if required by law.

6. Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use basic cookies or analytics tools to understand general visitor behavior and improve user experience. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information submitted to us. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Any future application form or client portal will be connected to a secure, authenticated backend system rather than storing data client-side.

8. International Data Transfers

As an agency serving international clients across the US, UK, Australia, France, and Kuwait, information may be processed in jurisdictions other than your country of residence. We take steps to handle information responsibly regardless of location.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details on our Contact page.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for business professionals, authors, and publishers. We do not knowingly collect information from individuals under the age of 18.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

12. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be directed to us via our Contact page.
